About Me

I am a computational and theoretical physicist specializing in the development of numerical methods to describe quantum many-body systems and quantum impurity phenomena.

Currently pursuing my PhD at the São Carlos Institute of Physics (USP), I research time-dependent X-ray absorption, photoemission, and quantum transport in metals. I also conducted research as a visiting scholar at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität (LMU) in Munich, Germany.

Research Interests:

Many-Body Physics Matrix Product States (MPS/DMRG) Numerical Renormalization Group (NRG) Kondo Effect & Heavy Fermions Quantum Phase Transitions Non-Equilibrium Quantum Dynamics
